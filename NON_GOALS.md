# Product non-goals

Content Center produces an editorial specification and production hand-off. It does not render video, replace an NLE, automate CapCut or Premiere, host source footage, scrape private platforms, or publish content automatically.

The system may later export structured Markdown, JSON, or editor-friendly tables. Any future media integration must preserve the current source-of-truth boundary: the content pack remains reviewable and portable without requiring a proprietary render pipeline.

Accounts, cloud sync, and collaboration are not implied by the local-first interface. They require a separate approved backend and security design.
