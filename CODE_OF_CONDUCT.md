# Code of Conduct

Content Center is a public project for creators, editors, AI users, and software contributors. Participants are expected to help maintain a respectful, practical, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate with respect, assume good intent while addressing impact, provide actionable feedback, protect other people’s private information, and keep discussions focused on improving the product for creators and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sharing private credentials or personal data, deliberate disruption, and unwanted sexual or hateful content are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit, remove, or close contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be made privately through the repository’s available security or contact channels rather than debated publicly.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, documentation, and other project-managed spaces.
